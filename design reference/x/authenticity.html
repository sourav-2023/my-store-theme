The Promise

Verified Authentic

Every piece sold by The Wardrobe is verified authentic. Here's exactly how we do it — and what you can expect when you buy from us.

Our process

01

Source from trusted channels

Flagship boutiques, brand SNKRS releases, verified resellers (StockX, GOAT), and personal contacts in NYC, LA, Tokyo, Dubai, and Singapore.

02

Hands-on inspection

Every shoe, garment, and accessory is physically inspected against brand reference materials — stitching, hardware, weight, smell, packaging, paperwork.

03

Provenance documented

We record where each piece came from, when, and ship it with all original accessories — box, dust bag, receipt, certificate, where applicable.

04

Lifetime guarantee

If a piece is ever proven inauthentic, we refund in full — no expiration. We've never had to. We don't intend to.

What "Verified Authentic" means on a listing

Every product page carries a gold "Verified Authentic" badge. That badge is our promise: this piece was inspected by us, sourced from a documented channel, and would pass any third-party authentication service.

If you'd like to see source documentation for a specific piece — receipts, photos of original packaging, brand certificates — just message us on WhatsApp and ask.
